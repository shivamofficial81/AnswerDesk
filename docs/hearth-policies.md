# Hearth & Co. Customer Policies

Hearth & Co. is an online home-goods retailer based in Portland, Oregon,
shipping to customers across the United States and select international
destinations. This document is the source of truth for customer-facing
policies. Last updated: March 3, 2026.

## Shipping

Standard Shipping (5–8 business days): $6.95, or **free on orders over
$75** before tax. Expedited Shipping (2–3 business days): $14.95 flat
rate. Overnight Shipping (1 business day): $29.95 flat rate, available
for orders placed before 1:00 PM Pacific Time on business days.

Orders are processed and leave our Portland, OR warehouse within 1–2
business days. Business days are Monday–Friday, excluding federal
holidays. Once a package ships, you'll receive a tracking number by
email within 24 hours.

We currently ship to all 50 U.S. states, plus Puerto Rico and the U.S.
Virgin Islands. Orders to Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, and the U.S.
Virgin Islands add a flat $12.00 surcharge on top of the standard rate
and may take an additional 3–5 business days.

**International shipping:** We ship to Canada and the United Kingdom
only. Canadian orders: flat $24.95, 7–12 business days. UK orders: flat
$34.95, 10–15 business days. International customers are responsible
for any customs duties, import taxes, or brokerage fees charged by
their country; these are not collected at checkout.

Oversized items (furniture over 50 lbs, rugs larger than 8x10) ship via
a separate freight carrier and include a $45.00–$95.00 shipping fee
calculated at checkout based on destination and weight. These items are
not eligible for free shipping promotions.

## Returns

We accept returns within **30 days of the delivery date** for a full
refund to your original payment method, and within **31–60 days** for
store credit only. Returns initiated after 60 days cannot be accepted.

To qualify for a return, items must be unused, in their original
packaging, and in resellable condition, with all tags and inserts
included. Items showing signs of use, assembly, or damage from customer
handling will be refused or subject to a 20% restocking fee.

**How to return:** Start a return request at hearthandco.com/returns
using your order number and the email address used at checkout. We'll
email a prepaid return shipping label; a $6.95 label fee is deducted
from your refund unless the return is due to our error (wrong item
shipped, damaged in transit, or defective on arrival), in which case
the label is free.

**Exclusions — final sale, not eligible for return:**
- Clearance and "Final Sale" tagged items
- Custom or made-to-order items (including monogrammed or custom-cut
  textiles)
- Candles, diffusers, and other opened consumable/scented goods
- Mattresses and pillows once the hygiene seal is removed
- Gift cards

## Refunds

Once we receive and inspect a returned item (typically within 3
business days of arrival at our returns center), refunds are issued to
the original payment method within 5–7 business days. Shipping charges
from the original order are non-refundable except when the return is
due to our error. Store credit issued in lieu of refund never expires
and is delivered by email as a gift card code within 1 business day of
inspection.

## Damaged or Missing Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or with items missing,
contact us within **7 days of delivery** at support@hearthandco.com
with your order number and photos of the item and its packaging. We
will send a free replacement at no cost, or issue a full refund
including original shipping, whichever you prefer. Claims submitted
after 7 days are reviewed on a case-by-case basis and are not
guaranteed a replacement.

For freight-delivered furniture, note any visible damage on the
delivery receipt at the time of drop-off when possible — this speeds up
the claims process but is not required to file a claim.

## Warranty

All Hearth & Co. furniture carries a **1-year limited warranty**
against manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship, starting
from the delivery date. Small appliances and electronics (lamps,
diffusers, kitchen gadgets) carry a **90-day limited warranty**.
Textiles, dinnerware, glassware, and decor items are not covered by
warranty and are handled under the standard return/damaged-item
policies above.

The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, damage from improper
assembly, damage from use outside normal household conditions, or
cosmetic changes like fading from sunlight exposure. To file a warranty
claim, email support@hearthandco.com with your order number, a
description of the defect, and photos. Approved warranty claims are
resolved with a free repair, replacement part, full replacement item,
or refund, at Hearth & Co.'s discretion.

## Order Tracking and Changes

Track your order any time at hearthandco.com/track using your order
number and email, or via the tracking link sent by email once your
order ships.

Order changes (shipping address, item swaps, cancellations) can be
made within **1 hour of placing your order** by contacting
support@hearthandco.com — after that window, orders enter fulfillment
and cannot be modified or canceled. If a change request comes in after
the 1-hour window, we recommend waiting for delivery and starting a
standard return instead.

## Payment Methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple
Pay, and Google Pay. We do not accept personal checks, money orders, or
cryptocurrency. Payment is charged in full at the time of order
placement, not at shipment. Hearth & Co. gift cards can be purchased in
denominations of $25, $50, $100, and $250, and never expire.
